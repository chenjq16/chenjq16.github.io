Sugamo is home to several excellent ramen shops, each with its own distinctive style. Here are three of my favorites, all within easy walking distance of Sugamo Station.

## Menya Imamura

Menya Imamura specializes in ramen made with chicken and dried sardines. It is located just south of Sugamo Station at 1-13-3 Sugamo, Toshima City.

[:round_pushpin: Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%E9%BA%BA%E3%82%84+%E3%81%84%E3%81%BE%E6%9D%91+%E5%B7%A3%E9%B4%A8)

## RAMEN and TSUKEMEN Number.6

As its name suggests, Number.6 offers both ramen and tsukemen. The shop is north of Sugamo Station at Brown Tower 1F, 2-1-2 Sugamo, Toshima City.

[:round_pushpin: Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=RAMEN+and+TSUKEMEN+Number.6+%E5%B7%A3%E9%B4%A8)

## Kamo to Negi Sugamo

Kamo to Negi is known for its duck-and-scallion ramen. The Sugamo branch is south of the station at Sky Tower Annex 1F, 1-11-4 Sugamo, Toshima City.

[:round_pushpin: Open in Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=%E3%82%89%E3%83%BC%E3%82%81%E3%82%93+%E9%B4%A8%26%E8%91%B1+%E5%B7%A3%E9%B4%A8%E5%BA%97)

Opening hours and menus may change, so it is worth checking the latest information on Google Maps before visiting.
