Jianqiang Chen is a doctoral student in Precision Engineering at the University of Tokyo, where he conducts research in the Mobile Robotics
Laboratory. His research focuses on robotic motion planning, learning from demonstration, and reinforcement learning, with the broader goal
of enabling robots to perform complex tasks efficiently and reliably. He is a recipient of the University of Tokyo Fellowship.

Before joining the University of Tokyo, Jianqiang received an MS in Electronic and Information Engineering and a BS in Measurement and Control
Technology and Instrument from Tsinghua University, where he also completed a second bachelor's degree in Economics. His earlier research
focused on pedestrian inertial navigation, including embedded navigation systems, motion-state recognition, and human-motion constraints.
He has also worked on sensing systems and biomedical devices. His research has appeared in *IEEE Robotics and Automation Letters*, *Sensors*,
and IEEE international conferences on electronics and instrumentation technologies.
